# Trademark Policy

**Electrik**, **Slate**, associated logos, and domain names (including electrik.dev and slate.electrik.dev) are trademarks or brand identifiers of {{ config('legal.operator') }} and the Electrik project ("we", "us").

This policy explains acceptable use. It does not replace the [software license](/license/) for Electrik code or the MIT license for Slate.

## Permitted uses

You may:

- State truthfully that your product is **built with Electrik** or **uses Slate**, without implying official endorsement
- Link to our official sites and GitHub repositories
- Use screenshots of our demo or documentation for review, education, or comparison
- Refer to Electrik/Slate by name in documentation, blog posts, and conference talks

## Uses that require permission

Contact [{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}](mailto:{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}) before:

- Using our logos in your product UI, app store listing, or marketing as if you are Electrik
- Registering domain names, social handles, or company names that include "Electrik" or "Slate" in a confusing way
- Implying partnership, sponsorship, or certification by Electrik when none exists
- Selling merchandise bearing our marks

## Prohibited uses

You may not:

- Use our trademarks in a way that suggests we endorse or guarantee your product
- Modify our logos or combine them with marks that could cause confusion
- Use our trademarks in violation of applicable law

## Open source and trademarks

The BSL and MIT licenses grant rights in **source code**, not in **trademarks**. You may fork and modify code under the applicable license; you may not rebrand a derivative as "official Electrik" or "official Slate."

## Enforcement

We may request changes to misleading uses. Repeated or bad-faith misuse may result in legal action.
