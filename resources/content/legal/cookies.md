# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how [electrik.dev](https://electrik.dev) uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also refer to similar technologies (such as local storage or analytics identifiers) as "cookies" for simplicity.

## Cookies we use

**Strictly necessary.** Required for basic site operation (for example session or security-related cookies if enabled).

**Analytics.** We may use **Google Analytics** (or similar) to measure traffic and usage in aggregate. These cookies help us understand which pages are useful. Analytics is only loaded when configured for production.

**Preferences.** Theme preference (light/dark) may be stored locally in your browser so the site remembers your choice.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on this marketing site.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect theme persistence or other minor features.

To opt out of Google Analytics across sites, see [Google's tools](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
