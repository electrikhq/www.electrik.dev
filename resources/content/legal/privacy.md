# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Electrik** ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you visit [electrik.dev](https://electrik.dev), read our documentation, or contact us about the Electrik product.

## What we collect

**Information you provide.** If you email us (for example about a commercial license, support, or studio inquiry), we receive your message, email address, and any details you choose to include.

**Usage data.** We may use analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand traffic to this site — for example pages viewed, approximate location, browser type, and referral source. See our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies/).

**GitHub and package registries.** If you install Electrik via Composer or interact with our GitHub repositories, those platforms handle data under their own policies. We do not receive your identity from Packagist or GitHub unless you contact us directly.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Respond to inquiries and license requests
- Operate, secure, and improve the website and documentation
- Understand aggregate usage of marketing pages
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

## Legal bases (where applicable)

If you are in a region that requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on legitimate interests (site operation and security), contract performance (when you purchase or request a license), and consent where required (for example non-essential cookies where applicable).

## Retention

We keep correspondence and transaction records as long as needed for support, licensing, accounting, and legal compliance, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

## Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, analytics, email). They may only use it to provide services to us. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Your choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you by emailing [{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}](mailto:{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}). You can control cookies through your browser; see the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies/).
