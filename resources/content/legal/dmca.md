# DMCA Copyright Policy

Electrik respects intellectual property rights. If you believe content on our websites or GitHub repositories infringes your copyright, send a notice as described below.

**Designated agent:** {{ config('legal.operator') }}  
**Email:** [{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}](mailto:{{ config('legal.contact_email') }}?subject=DMCA%20Notice)

## Required information

Your notice should include:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed
2. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (URL, file path, commit, or repository)
3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone, email)
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
6. Your physical or electronic signature

## Counter-notification

If you believe material was removed in error, you may send a counter-notification with the information required under applicable law (including consent to jurisdiction and identification of removed material).

## Repeat infringers

We may terminate access for users who repeatedly infringe copyright where appropriate.

## Note on GitHub

Much of our source code is hosted on GitHub. You may also file notices through [GitHub's DMCA process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/submitting-content-removal-requests).
