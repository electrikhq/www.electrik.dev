# Teamwork

Teams are powered by `electrik/teamwork`, Electrik's maintained fork of the Teamwork package (Laravel-ready).

You normally consume it **through Electrik** — treat it as the teams engine under the hood, not a separate product to market beside Electrik and Slate.
